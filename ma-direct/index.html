Legacy vs Current

Setting shifts for a slice

When an enterprise client needs shelf inspections across the whole country, I want tasks to adapt automatically to each geo slice (state, city, district), so I avoid most manual work.

I designed a calendar with easily configurable nested nodes: a task location sits in a district, districts sit in a city, cities sit in areas, and areas sit in countries. A manager can pick a slice, such as a city district, to create a schedule and assign workers in bulk.

The first alternative was a visual map, but it requires effort to map zones and is hard to scale and support. The second was an auto algorithm, but it is overkill for irregular activities and beyond team resources.

Manager task time dropped from 6–7 hours to an estimated 45 minutes. I also removed Excel imports and exports and the manual work with a plain list of shops. The client also wins because he gets his reports faster.
