Outcomes

After EU sanctions hit the RU business, the bank kept running mission-critical internal workflows, even though it couldn’t pay for Western vendor software.

Situation

The bank uses third-party tools to process secured loan applications. Since developers cannot update the software directly, they can’t apply the latest technologies: machine learning collateral appraisal, cloud storage, seamless staff experience, and more. As a result, time-to-yes and time-to-collateral metrics are slowing, NPS is poor, and vendor software costs too much.
