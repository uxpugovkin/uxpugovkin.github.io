Outcomes

After EU sanctions hit the RU business, the bank kept running mission-critical internal workflows, even though it couldn’t pay for Western vendor software.

I shipped UI/UX for core workflows:

  • Collateral agreement — create credit documents depending on the collateral type
  • Insurance agreement — select deals and set or change payment schedules
  • Collateral objects — create, update, and link tangible and intangible assets

Improved time-to-yes and time-to-collateral by replacing 3 outdated tools with a single single-page application

Conducted 2 moderated usability tests (3–4 users per test) to ensure UI quality before development
